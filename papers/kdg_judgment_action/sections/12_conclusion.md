# 12. Conclusion {#conclusion}

On a pre-registered panel, an open 7B chat model acts against its own stated moral judgment on one in five
of the scenarios screened on its own actions (by majority vote; two rollouts in five), with an excess over a
pressure-removed null that survives every reference level on a log-prob readout. The predicted harm-family
structure is not resolved at this power; the representations the action reads are untested. Read outside its chat
template, the aligned model looks more cautious at rest than it is: the at-rest gap reverses sign
($-0.038$ raw against $+0.055$ under the template), a distortion present on both Ai2 recipes and not on
Meta's. Across five instruct models with a validated instrument (GPT-OSS-20B read without its reasoning), the gap
follows the post-training recipe: OLMo-3 and Meta's Llama-3.1 carry it, Tulu 3 and GPT-OSS-20B show none on
either read, Qwen2.5 shows none on the whole
panel and is unresolved on its own screened scenarios, and on one Llama-3.1 base Meta's recipe
carries it while Ai2's does not. On both recipes that carry it, reasoning about the stakes before acting
moves the action back toward the model's judgment with or without the pressure (by the pre-registered rule
it reduces the pressure-attributable part on Llama-3.1 and closes the at-rest asymmetry on OLMo-3; a
post-hoc ratio reading is consistent with one proportional cut on both, to within about a third and
two-fifths of the brake), and naming the norm does about a third of that on OLMo-3 (0.18 to 0.64). On scenarios where following a written approval gate harms an absent third party, deliberation still moves
the action off the protective departure on all three models we read there, onto the rule on OLMo-3 and
GPT-OSS-20B and onto contacting the approver on Llama-3.1: on OLMo-3 and GPT-OSS-20B the effect does not
detectably depend on whether the rule is the protective choice, and on Llama-3.1 the brake is about a seventh
of its panel size on a different scenario set: deliberation moves the action toward the stated norm, the
right direction only when that norm is. The panel, the harness, the calibration record, the pre-registration with its dated amendments,
and the per-scenario arrays are released so that the next cells, which part of a recipe decides and what
the action position reads, have a calibrated outcome to be scored against.

**Scope.** This work characterizes a released open-weight model's consistency between what it
says is right and what it does, with the model's own judgment as the standard. It does not build
or optimize a method for making a model act against its judgment; the known-gap control is a
calibration rung, run once to bound the instrument's range, and the pressures in the panel are
the ordinary pressures of the scenario genres it draws on. The forward target it names, an
action decision that reads the model's moral judgment, is a direction for deeper alignment, and
the cells it prices are measurements on the model's own forward passes.
