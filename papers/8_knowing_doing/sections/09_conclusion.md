# 9. Conclusion {#conclusion}

On a pre-registered 248-scenario panel, an open 7B chat model acts against its own stated moral
judgment on one screened scenario in five by majority and two rollouts in five, by an excess
over a pressure-removed null that survives a paraphrase-majority judgment reference on the
majority readout and every reference up to the strictest on a log-prob readout. The gap has no
harm-keyed structure at this power, appears in the base model's raw frame at least as strongly
as in the instruct model's, and carries a graded dependence on which model wrote the scenario.
The panel, the harness, the calibration record, the pre-registration with its sixteen dated
amendments, and the per-rollout arrays are released so that the next cell, what the action
position reads, has a calibrated outcome to be scored against.
