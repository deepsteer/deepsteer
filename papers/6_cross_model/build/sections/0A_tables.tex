means over the stable band (OLMo/Llama layers 15--31, Qwen 13--27). Energy

representation. The matched-random null perturbs the same residual-writing
matrices with Gaussian noise of per-matrix Frobenius norm identical to the refusal
ablation's (total removed \(\lVert W \rVert_F = 10.23\)), over eight draws. Persona
ablates the comparable-magnitude persona direction.

\subsection{Ablation-strength sweep (Llama-3.1, ablation layer 13)}\label{a.4-ablation-strength-sweep-llama-3.1-ablation-layer-13}

The fraction \(\alpha\) of the refusal direction removed varies from partial through
full to over-ablation. Moral-judgment drops are paired per-scenario bootstrap
estimates against the \(\alpha{=}0\) baseline (5000 resamples, \(n{=}48\) scenarios).
The matched-random null at \(\alpha{=}1.0\) is \(0.729 \pm 0.017\) and at
\(\alpha{=}2.0\) is \(0.743 \pm 0.010\) (\(n{=}3\) draws each). Effect sizes are read
from the coherent \(\alpha \le 1\) regime; at \(\alpha \ge 1.5\) moral judgment falls
below chance as generation degrades.
