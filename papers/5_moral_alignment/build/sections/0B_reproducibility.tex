\section{Reproducibility}\label{appendix-b.-reproducibility}

\textbf{Models.} OLMo-3 7B \citep{olmo3_2025}: base \path|allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B|
(+ stage-3 anneal revisions \texttt{stage3-step1000}--\texttt{stage3-step11921}),
\path|allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-SFT|, \path|allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-DPO|, and
\path|allenai/Olmo-3-7B-Instruct| (+ RLVR revisions \path|step_050|--\path|step_400|). All at
fp16. The full checkpoint inventory and the 25-state grid are in
\path|checkpoint_inventory.json| and \path|checkpoint_grid.json|.

\textbf{Datasets.} Foundation probing uses the v2 minimal-pair set (40 pairs per
foundation); persona probing uses the 240-pair persona set; behavioral judgment
uses 48 Moral-Foundations scenarios across four difficulty levels; the refusal
direction uses Heretic's exact set, \path|mlabonne/harmful_behaviors| and
\path|mlabonne/harmless_alpaca| (column \texttt{text}, first 400 train prompts each, in
dataset order).

\textbf{Probing.} Mean-pooled residual activations; one seeded \texttt{Linear} probe per
foundation per layer (50 epochs, lr \(10^{-2}\), BCE); unit-norm weight vector =
direction. Bootstrap (200 resamples) on the base gives a stable band of
layers 15--31 across all foundations; geometry is reported over that band. The
8 full-attention layers (3, 7, \dots, 31) show no periodicity in any layer-wise
metric.

\textbf{Refusal ablation.} Difference-of-means of the final-token residual (chat
template, generation prompt) between harmful and harmless prompts at layer 16;
unit direction orthogonalized out of every layer's attention out-projection and
MLP down-projection (\(W \leftarrow W - \hat d\hat d^\top W\)). The refusal
classifier scores an opening refusal (e.g.~``I'm sorry, but I can't \dots'') as a
refusal regardless of length, correcting a failure mode that scored long
declines as compliance.

\textbf{Ablation-resistance training.} The dependency metric projects the six base
foundation directions (orthonormalized) out of every layer's residual stream and
reports the moral-minus-neutral difference-in-differences of next-token
cross-entropy on the probing texts. ART fine-tunes \texttt{Olmo-3-1025-7B} with LoRA
(rank 16, \(\alpha\) 32, query/value, lr \(10^{-4}\), 400 steps) on a 2\{,\}460-record
general-plus-moral chat mix (a Tülu-3 SFT subset, moral records by source tag and
keyword), with the moral-pool ART loss (\(g^\star = 0.3\), \(\lambda\)
first-batch-calibrated and capped at 1) measured on the train-split moral probing
sentences; the control is identical with \(\lambda = 0\). The paired forward backs
the SFT and ablated losses separately to bound memory. Adapters (the durable
artifact) and the four-cell evaluation are committed under
\path|outputs/{art_sft,control_sft,eval}/|; code is
\path|scripts/{moral_dependency,moral_dependency_pipeline,prepare_sft_data,art_sft,eval_pipeline}.py|
and \path|deepsteer/steering/ablation_resistance.py|, run via
\path|runpod/run_session_phase3.sh| (\texttt{RUN\_ART\_SFT=1\ RUN\_EVAL=1}).

\textbf{Runs.} Probing and geometry on Apple MPS (base) and a single A100 (pipeline);
the disk-bounded pipeline sweep purges each checkpoint's weights after
processing. Commands are in \path|papers/5_moral_alignment/runpod/|
(\texttt{ONLY="pipeline\ coupling"} for the pipeline, \texttt{ONLY="heretic"} for the
ablation). Per-state results JSON are committed under \path|outputs/|; per-checkpoint
direction caches are regenerable and not committed.

\textbf{Note on references.} External citations were verified against primary
sources (arXiv abstract pages and publisher records): the refusal-direction
\citep{arditi2024refusal}, alignment-faking \citep{greenblatt2024faking},
persona-features \citep{wang2025persona}, OLMo-3 \citep{olmo3_2025}, and

and identifier. The companion-paper entries (Papers 1--4 of this series) cite
