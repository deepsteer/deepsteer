\section{Supplementary tables}\label{appendix-a.-supplementary-tables}

Table \ref{tab:transfer} reports the comprehension trajectory: base-trained
foundation directions stay near-perfect classifiers (AUC \(\approx 1.0\)) and full
probe accuracy at every state, while the cosine between the base directions and
each state's freshly fitted directions crystallizes during pre-training and
drops once at SFT. Table \ref{tab:coupling} reports the coupling battery on the
instruct-capable states and the ablated model. Refusal--morality cosines
(layer 16): Care \(0.074\), Fairness \(0.062\), Liberty \(0.068\), Loyalty \(0.061\),
Authority \(0.038\), Sanctity \(0.059\); moral-subspace projection fraction \(0.104\);
mean \(|\cos| = 0.061\).
