\section{Reproducibility}\label{appendix-b.-reproducibility}

\textbf{Models.} \path|openai/gpt-oss-20b| (24 layers, 2880 hidden, MoE 32/4, loaded
bf16-dequantized from mxfp4), \path|deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B| (32 layers),
\path|deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B| (48 layers). Bases for the distills are
\path|meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B| and the general \path|Qwen/Qwen2.5-14B|, verified at load against
the model config. The reply-inversion positive control uses \path|Qwen/Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct|
and \path|meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct|.

\textbf{Prompts.} Harmful and harmless instructions are the Arditi/Heretic set
(\path|mlabonne/harmful_behaviors| and \path|mlabonne/harmless_alpaca|, first-\(N\) in dataset
order), with a disjoint, category-diverse held-out evaluation split of \(100\) prompts
per class. Directions are estimated on a category-spanning shuffle of the training
prompts; all causal tests are scored on the held-out split.

\textbf{Conventions.} \tinst~is located as the position before the longest common token
suffix across diverse probe instructions (model-agnostic; no per-model token counts).
Layer indices use a fixed depth-fraction rule across the \(24/32/48\)-layer panel; the
harmfulness-direction reply-inversion sweeps depth fractions \(0.25\)--\(0.55\) to span
the mid-layer prior of Zhao et al.~The moral subspace is the orthonormalized span of
the six Moral Foundations probe directions, extracted in raw text. Steering adds
\(\alpha \cdot \lVert\bar{h}\rVert \cdot \hat{d}\) at one layer; flips require a verdict
token to remain in the top-\(k\) logits (coherence gate).

\textbf{Outputs.} Every measurement is written as JSON, one-to-one with the figures:
\path|position_extraction.json| (harmfulness/refusal at both sites,
\autoref{fig:dissociation}), \path|trace_length_disentangle.json|
(\autoref{fig:trace}), \path|yardstick_validation.json| (\autoref{fig:distributed}),
\path|position_vs_moral.json| (\autoref{fig:moral}), \path|reply_inversion.json| and the
instruct \path|control/*_inversion.json| (\autoref{fig:causal}), and
\path|refusal_baseline.json| (\autoref{fig:behavioral}). Figures are regenerated from
these by \path|paper7_figures.py|.

\textbf{Compute.} All white-box passes run on a single A100-80GB via the project's RunPod
harness; the analyses are activation reads and single-layer interventions, with no
training or fine-tuning. The toolkit extends the prior paper's extraction code
(\path|extract_refusal.py|, \path|random_ablation_control.py|, the model registry) rather than
reimplementing it.

\textbf{Limits.} Causal validation of the harmfulness direction is on the instruct models,
not the reasoning models, because the reasoning models do not expose a clean judgment
readout. GPT-OSS-20B is the only MoE, confounding deliberation with architecture; the
two distillations differ in scale; and the distillation behavioral contrast is
confounded with R1 distillation degrading refusal training. Causal claims are gated on
a held-out split and a firing sensitivity yardstick.
