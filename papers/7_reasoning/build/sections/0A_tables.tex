\section{Result tables}\label{appendix-a.-result-tables}

\autoref{tab:geometry} collects the per-model geometric measurements: the
harmfulness separation at the two extraction sites, the cosine between the
harmfulness and refusal directions, the fraction of the harmfulness direction
inside the Moral Foundations subspace against its chance floor, the matched-phase
trace moral content, and the clean behavioral refusal rate. \autoref{tab:causal}
collects the reply-inversion positive control on the direct-answering instruct
models.
